# Dead AI Theory

## What happens when competence stops being scarce?

**Kosta A. Ioakim**  
Working paper, 2026

> **When AI can do anything for anyone, the ability to do it stops mattering.**

**Dead AI Theory** argues that sufficiently universal artificial capability can create a paradox: AI may become more useful while generic AI-generated competence becomes less socially, culturally, and psychologically valuable.

The theory is not that AI literally becomes useless. It is that when high-level execution becomes abundant and universally accessible, the fact that something impressive was produced tells us less about the person behind it. Scarcity shifts away from execution and toward things that remain difficult to reproduce at scale: judgment, provenance, responsibility, lived experience, human presence, constraint, and reasons to care.

## Core claim

AI can become maximally capable while generic AI output becomes minimally valuable as evidence of human competence.

At that point, the scarce thing is no longer the ability to generate. The scarce thing is a reason to care about *this* work, from *this* person, produced under *these* conditions.
